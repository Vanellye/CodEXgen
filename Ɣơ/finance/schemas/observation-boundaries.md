# Observation data boundaries

These records are not financial evidence.

- Pathology/water: sample identity, collection time, method, units, measured values, uncertainty, laboratory/source reference, chain of custody. Measurements are not diagnoses.
- Shower: fixture/device identifier, flow rate, temperature, duration, estimated water volume, calibration, timestamp.
- Fire/environment: sensor/event identifier, temperature, smoke/air-quality observations, calibration, timestamp, provenance. No unattended physical actuation.

Do not merge these schemas with balances, deposits, insurance markers, or withdrawal authorization. Cross-domain joins require explicit purpose, consent, and documented mapping.
