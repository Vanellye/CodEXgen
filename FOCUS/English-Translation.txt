SAVEs PRINCIPLE
===============
SAVEs means: "Save All Victory's Everything."

Founding statement:
"Preserve the victory. Preserve the journey. Preserve the source."

Preserve achievements, failed attempts, repairs, evidence, and their lineage
without overwriting original history. The marker "ị/" is a diagnostic
lineage/story marker. It is not a replacement for valid source syntax or
functional newline escapes.
