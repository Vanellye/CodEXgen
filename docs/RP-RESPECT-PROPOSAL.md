# RP — Respect Proposal

**Purpose:** An open-ended creation channel for proposals, experiments, improvements, research notes, and collaborative implementation ideas.

## Meaning
**RP** stands for **Respect Proposal**. Respect means preserving the origin and intent of contributions, making assumptions visible, and allowing ideas to evolve without claiming ownership of another person's mind or work.

## Open scope
This proposal remains open for continuing creation. Add dated sections or linked implementation branches as work develops. It is not a one-time feature specification and does not imply that every proposal is accepted or implemented.

## Contribution record
For each contribution, record:
- **Origin:** who or what supplied it, with a source link where available
- **Type:** observation, interpretation, proposal, question, or implementation
- **Intent:** the problem or possibility being explored
- **Evidence:** supporting material and known uncertainty
- **Next step:** experiment, discussion, implementation, defer, or reject
- **Status:** proposed, exploring, validated, implemented, or published

## Working principles
1. Preserve provenance and retain source lineage.
2. Distinguish evidence from interpretation and aspiration.
3. Make uncertainty, limitations, and failed experiments visible.
4. Keep changes scoped, traceable, and reversible.
5. Do not place credentials, private keys, or personal secrets in the repository.
6. Use feature branches; do not force-push or silently overwrite history.
7. A proposal, PR merge, successful build, and external publication are distinct states.

## Publication boundary
This RP is a public repository proposal. Opening it does not authorize an external dispatch, release, financial action, or production deployment. Such destinations and triggers must be explicitly identified and auditable. No automatic publication workflow is enabled by this charter alone.

## Continuation
Contributors may append proposals and link focused implementation PRs here. Keep this RP open as the continuing index; merge individual implementation work separately when its own completion criteria are met.

## CodEXgen automatic-publication objective

The project owner has requested automatic publication as an RP objective. Before enabling an automation, record the exact publication target, artifact types, triggering event, credentials/permissions, validation gates, receipt format, rollback path, and whether publication is public or internal. Do not infer an external destination or silently dispatch to a ledger, service, or third-party system. Keep main protected and use isolated branches.
