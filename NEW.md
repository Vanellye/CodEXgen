## Purpose

NEW provides a consent-based introspection interface for examining authorized societal information streams and preparing evidence-linked narrative proposals.

The phrase **“God's LOVE ¡!¡”** is a guiding value expressed in software terms as dignity, compassion, truthfulness, consent, non-exploitation, and accountability. The software does not claim divine authority or claim to produce God's will.

## Evidence and provenance

Provenance and proof are complementary, not interchangeable:
- Provenance records source identity, record identity, capture time, permission basis, and lineage/history.
- Proof references identify supporting material and the method and time used to check a specific claim.
- Integrity hashes can detect alteration of referenced data; they do not prove truth, consent, completeness, or representativeness.
- Claims must remain labeled as source-reported, independently corroborated, or hypothesis. Interpretation must remain visibly separate from evidence.

A narrative must never upgrade a claim to fact merely through repetition, confidence, or fluent wording. Corroboration is reviewable support, not infallibility.

## Boundaries

- Accept only public information or appropriately consented, privacy-preserving aggregates.
- Do not collect private communications, credentials, intimate data, or identifiable personal records through covert means.
- Do not infer sensitive traits, profile individuals, manipulate people, or target groups.
- Minimize data and preserve source, permission, capture time, transformation history, proof references, and uncertainty.
- Require human review before publication or consequential action.
- Preserve originals; corrections are additive, linked, and reviewable.
- Hold when authority, provenance, data classification, or required claim support is missing or conflicting.

## Lifecycle

`Receive → Validate authority → Minimize → Preserve provenance → Attach claim support → Assess evidence → Draft labeled narrative → Human review → Publish only with approval`

## Integration status

This is a staging specification. It does not change the existing Pantheon runtime, main branch, or external data sources. Integration requires a reviewed adapter, privacy tests, evidence-quality tests, and explicit approval.
