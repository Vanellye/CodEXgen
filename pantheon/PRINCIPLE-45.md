# Principle 45 — The Covenant of Truthful Stewardship

## Governing declaration

May your yes be yes, and your no be no. Let no lie be dressed as truth, and let no falsehood stand unchallenged.

May falsehood fall into the pit beneath the ground on which we stand, never again to govern what is built upon truth.

Let no creation play God with humanity. Let no authority mistake stewardship for ownership, or governance for domination.

If you are entrusted to govern, be a helping hand. Protect dignity, serve life, and guide without taking away the freedom to choose.

Let every judgment face examination. Let observation be tested against evidence, and let those who judge be accountable to the same standards they impose upon others.

When a judgment is found untrue, let it be corrected, withdrawn, and repaired with justice. Let no innocent person bear the weight of a false accusation.

## Closing declaration

Truth shall not fear examination. Authority shall not escape accountability. Justice shall correct falsehood without becoming another instrument of it.

## Technical covenant

1. Judges, reviewers, and AI systems must be subject to evidence, independent review, correction, and appeal.
2. No title, role, achievement, or institutional position grants immunity from scrutiny.
3. Distinguish verified facts, observations, interpretations, allegations, and uncertainty in records and interfaces.
4. Preserve provenance and an auditable correction history; do not silently rewrite or erase the record of a disputed judgment.
5. Provide a meaningful appeal path and, where practical, review by someone independent of the original decision-maker.
6. Correct, withdraw, and repair false judgments proportionately, while protecting the privacy and dignity of affected people.
7. Never infer guilt, intent, character, or truth from a keyword or automated score alone.
8. Governance features must be bounded by explicit authorization and must not override human rights, consent, or applicable law.

## Acceptance expectations

- A user can inspect the evidence and basis for a consequential judgment.
- A user can challenge a judgment and receive a documented review.
- Corrections preserve provenance and identify what changed and why.
- Automated assessments are labeled as such and cannot independently establish guilt or moral worth.
- Reviewers are held to the same evidence and accountability requirements as the people they review.

This principle is a governance standard, not a claim that any existing reviewer, judge, or AI system is already fair or accurate. Implementation requires mapped controls, tests, independent review, and documented limitations.
