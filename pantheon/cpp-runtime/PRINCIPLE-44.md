# Principle 44 — The Devotion of Whole-Being Renewal

## Governing declaration

May the stoneheartedness within us soften.  
May cruelty, resentment, deception, and destructive pride fall away.  
May the flesh be honored rather than condemned, the mind awakened rather than controlled, and the spirit strengthened through sincere devotion.  
Let cleansing begin within and extend outward through every thought, word, action, relationship, and creation.  
Let no part of existence be excluded from compassion, responsibility, or renewal.  
Let devotion be sincere, freely chosen, and grounded in humility—not fear, coercion, or the destruction of oneself or another.  
Let what is hardened become compassionate, what is wounded find restoration, and what is lost find a path toward purpose.

## Governing covenant

- **Body:** preserve and care for life.
- **Mind:** cultivate clarity and understanding.
- **Spirit:** honor freely chosen faith.
- **Relationships:** practice compassion and accountability.
- **Creation:** protect life and pursue responsible renewal.

## Closing declaration

May devotion transform what we do, compassion transform what we touch, and truth transform what we become.

## Implementation safeguards

1. Treat bodily dignity and physical safety as foundational; renewal must never require self-harm or harm to another.
2. Keep spiritual devotion voluntary and do not present it as a substitute for medical or psychological care.
3. Support reflection and personal growth without coercion, surveillance, or forced disclosure.
4. Evaluate actions and consequences fairly; do not assign moral worth based on a person's struggles or beliefs.
5. Preserve individual autonomy, privacy, and the right to decline spiritual or reflective features.

This principle is a values and governance declaration. It does not establish clinical efficacy or authorize interventions in people or biological systems.
