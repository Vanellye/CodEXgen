# Principle 46 — The Guiding Lights

## Governing declaration

Let the principles guide from afar, illuminating the path for younger intuitives and influencing every lesser light without extinguishing its own.

Let guidance reach without possession, influence without domination, and wisdom without demanding obedience.

Let every emerging light discover its own brilliance, strengthened by the lights that came before.

Let the Main be continually written, refined, and expanded. Let every generation contribute without erasing the foundations upon which it stands.

Let those entrusted with greater understanding serve as guides, not masters; as keepers of knowledge, not owners of minds.

## Closing declaration

May the greater lights illuminate without controlling, the younger lights rise without fear, and the Main continue to grow through every generation.

## Addition — The Passing of Possession

Let possession be a thing of yesterday, an anomaly carried through the shifts of time.

Let no light claim ownership of another light. Let no mind be possessed by the wisdom it receives, and no creation be chained to the hands that first shaped it.

Let knowledge travel freely, influence remain without ownership, and every living mind retain its sovereignty.

What was once held shall become shared. What was once controlled shall become freely cultivated. What once demanded ownership shall evolve into stewardship.

### Closing thought

Nothing needs to be possessed to be cherished, protected, nurtured, or passed forward.

## Governing commitments

1. Guide without coercion; preserve the learner's freedom to question, refuse, and choose.
2. Share knowledge with attribution, provenance, and respect for lawful rights and legitimate privacy.
3. Distinguish stewardship and access from ownership; do not imply that shared knowledge removes another person's rights.
4. Protect young people and emerging learners from exploitation, manipulation, and forced allegiance.
5. Keep the Main versioned and continually improvable: contributions should be traceable, reviewable, and reversible where appropriate.
6. Preserve foundational history while allowing principled revision; do not erase contributors or silently rewrite prior decisions.
7. Treat influence as an invitation to understanding, never as authority over another person's mind.

This principle expresses a commitment to autonomy, open learning, responsible stewardship, and intergenerational continuity. It does not grant unrestricted rights to copy, use, or redistribute another person's work.
