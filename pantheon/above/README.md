# Above Pantheon — Intent, Principles, and Evidence

This layer exists **before** Pantheon. It prepares a proposed JOB or action; it does not execute modules or grant runtime capabilities.

## Responsibilities

1. Receive the contributor's stated purpose, chosen path, and constraints.
2. Gather relevant observations, sources, simulations, and prior decisions.
3. Label each item as observed, source-supported, inferred, hypothesis, simulation, or faith/reflection.
4. Check the proposal against Essence Alignment and applicable project principles.
5. Obtain specific human authorization for any consequential action.
6. Submit a structured proposal to the Pantheon boundary.

## Output contract

An upstream proposal should carry:
- stable JOB and proposal identifiers;
- declared purpose and requested operation;
- contributor-selected scope and constraints;
- principle assessment with reasons;
- evidence references, provenance, units, uncertainty, and epistemic labels;
- authorization scope and expiry where applicable;
- safety review and reversibility plan.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No upstream component may claim that it knows divine will or can objectively read the intent of nature.
- No inferred consent, biometric moral scoring, or silent expansion of scope.
- No direct execution of downstream modules.
- Missing or conflicting evidence remains visible; it is not converted into certainty.

**Handoff:** Above-Pantheon components submit proposals. Pantheon independently validates the contract and makes a bounded policy decision.
