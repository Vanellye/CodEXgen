## Story — The Lantern That Kept Its Receipts
At the edge of a city that rebuilt itself every dawn, a small lantern recorded what it illuminated. It did not claim to own the streets, the people, or their memories. It kept only a trace: where its light fell, what remained uncertain, and who had asked it to shine. When the city council asked it to declare the whole world understood, the lantern answered: “I can show my path. I cannot replace your judgment.” The council made that sentence the first law of the archive.
