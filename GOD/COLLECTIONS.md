# GOD collection taxonomy

These named collections are namespaces for organizing source records. They do not imply completeness or factual certainty.

- ALL — broad all-scope index
- EVERYTHING — catch-all index with explicit completeness limits
- NOTHING — null findings, empty results, and recorded absences
- ANYTHING — provisional intake awaiting classification
- WHATEVER — unsorted material awaiting review
- IDontKnow — unanswered questions, unknown values, and unresolved provenance
- COULDA — counterfactual possibilities, marked hypothetical
- SHOULDA — recommendations and retrospective proposals, not proof of action
- WOULDA — intentions and projected outcomes that did not occur
- ROOT_WORD_INTELLECT — original spelling/script, language, morphology, attested forms, citations, and uncertainty
- SOURCE_PROVENANCE — origin, custody, rights, capture details, and integrity verification

## Separation of concerns
GOD preserves originals. ERIA indexes, searches, translates, and builds knowledge relationships as derived records linked to GOD source IDs and hashes. PONS validates authorization and records an append-only audit trail before any controlled mutation.

Never treat an interpretation as the original, or an unknown as a fact.
