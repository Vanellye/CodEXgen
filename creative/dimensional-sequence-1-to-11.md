# Dimensional Sequence: 1D–11D (conceptual art direction)

## Format
- Master: 3840x2160, 16:9, 24 fps, 10 seconds per stage, 110 seconds total.
- Social cut: 1080x1920, 9:16; recompose shots, do not merely crop.
- Inspect every rendered file with tools/video/inspect_video.py and store ffprobe JSON as technical provenance.
- These are visual metaphors for mathematical ideas, not evidence that higher physical dimensions have been observed.

## Eleven stages
1. **1D — Line / origin:** a single luminous point extends into a precise line; minimal black space, measured camera track.
2. **2D — Plane / relation:** the line unfolds into a coordinate plane; topology and intersections appear.
3. **3D — Volume / form:** the plane folds into a translucent geometric solid; parallax reveals depth.
4. **4D — Time / worldline:** a 3D object changes across time; temporal trails and synchronized motion.
5. **5D — Branching possibilities:** multiple alternate trajectories fork from one initial state; visualize choices, not literal universes.
6. **6D — Parameter space:** branches become a structured field of possible initial conditions; camera traverses a parameter lattice.
7. **7D — Geometry of families:** distinct parameter families form nested manifolds; layered translucent surfaces.
8. **8D — Transformation space:** forms deform through continuous transformations; morphs remain coherent and reversible.
9. **9D — Symmetry / invariants:** transformations reveal conserved patterns, repeating symmetries, and invariant structures.
10. **10D — Unified abstract space:** prior structures coexist in a single navigable symbolic topology; slow cinematic orbit.
11. **11D — Synthesis / horizon:** all prior motifs converge into a luminous, layered totality; end on a stable horizon and title.

## Continuity
Keep a persistent central seed motif, one consistent light source, and a gradual increase in geometric complexity. Use match cuts between the end geometry of one stage and the beginning of the next. Avoid textual claims that these are measured physical dimensions. Add labels in post-production for legible typography.
