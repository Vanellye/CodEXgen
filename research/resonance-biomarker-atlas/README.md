# ERIA — Resonance & Biological Systems Research Atlas

This folder holds the research companion for acoustic resonance/tuning, physiological signals, bilateral asymmetry, biomarker qualification, and evidence-led experimental design.

## Contents
- `ERIA-Resonance-Biomarker-Atlas.md` — full source manuscript (15-page PDF companion generated locally).
- `figures/` — original conceptual SVG diagrams. They are illustrations, not measurements.
- `build/README.md` — reproducible local PDF build instructions and output expectations.

## Scientific boundaries
“Resonate tuning” is used as a research/engineering term for calibrated input-response characterization, not as a universal healing frequency. “Asymmetrical bio markers” is treated as a proposed umbrella for measurable bilateral differences; it is not presented as a recognized standalone diagnostic category. The manuscript distinguishes established measurement methods from exploratory hypotheses and explicitly rejects unsupported claims of age reversal, telomere lengthening, or universal therapeutic effects from arbitrary frequencies.

## Provenance and governance
The figures were created for this atlas and are conceptual. No patient or animal data are included. Keep `main` protected; all changes are on `research/resonance-biomarker-atlas`. Review scientific updates, source licenses, and provenance before promotion.
