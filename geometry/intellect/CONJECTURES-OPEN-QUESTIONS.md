# Conjectures and Open Questions

**Purpose:** Keep unresolved mathematical ideas visible without presenting them as established results.

## Required labels

- **Conjecture:** a precise claim proposed for proof or disproof.
- **Open question:** a question not yet resolved within the stated scope.
- **Hypothesis for exploration:** a provisional assumption used to guide investigation; not necessarily a formal conjecture.
- **Counterexample search:** a deliberate attempt to find a case that disproves a claim.

## Review rules

1. Make claims precise enough to test.
2. State the domain and all assumptions.
3. Search for simple and degenerate counterexamples before attempting a broad proof.
4. Keep exploratory intuition separate from formal argument.
5. Record changes to the statement; do not silently replace an earlier conjecture.
6. Promote a conjecture to a theorem only after a valid proof has been reviewed.
