# Geometrical Intellect

This area contains the reasoning layer behind geometry and design: concepts, definitions, questions, axioms, derivations, proofs, interpretations, and documented design decisions.

## Include

- Mathematical definitions and notation
- Axioms, theorems, lemmas, and proofs
- Geometric reasoning and derivations
- Dimensional and coordinate-system explanations
- Clearly labeled conjectures and open questions
- Rationale explaining why a construction or design uses a particular geometry

## Keep separate

Do not store raw measurements, time-series records, sensor output, statistical plots, or empirical dashboards here. Link to the appropriate observation or experiment record instead.

## Evidence labels

- **Established:** supported by accepted mathematical definitions or results.
- **Derived:** follows from stated assumptions and a shown derivation.
- **Conjectural:** plausible but not proved.
- **Interpretive:** a conceptual reading or philosophical framing.
- **Illustrative:** an example used to communicate an idea, not evidence.

Prefer precise claims, explicit assumptions, and reproducible reasoning.
