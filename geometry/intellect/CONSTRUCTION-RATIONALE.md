# Construction and Design Rationale

**Purpose:** Explain why a construction or design uses a particular geometric model, rather than merely recording what it looks like.

## Rationale record

~~~text
Construction / design name:
Goal and intended use:
Chosen geometric framework:
Alternatives considered:
Constraints:
Why this geometry is suitable:
Properties relied upon:
Construction steps:
Trade-offs:
Known limitations:
Diagram / shape references:
Proof or derivation references:
Evidence status:
Revision notes:
~~~

## Questions to answer

- What problem is the construction intended to solve?
- Why is this geometry appropriate (for example, Euclidean, affine, projective, spherical, hyperbolic, or graph-based)?
- Which properties matter: distance, angle, parallelism, curvature, symmetry, connectivity, area, volume, or topology?
- Which properties are preserved by the chosen transformations?
- What assumptions make the construction valid?
- What alternatives were rejected, and why?
- Does the visual form faithfully represent the mathematical construction, or is it an artistic interpretation?

## Keep rationale distinct from proof

A design rationale explains a choice against stated goals and constraints. It may be well justified without being a theorem. If the rationale includes a mathematical claim, link to its proof or derivation. If it is aesthetic or interpretive, label it accordingly.
