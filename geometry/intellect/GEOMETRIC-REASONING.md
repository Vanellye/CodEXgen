# Geometric Reasoning and Derivations

**Purpose:** Preserve reproducible reasoning from a geometric question to a construction, formula, or conclusion.

## Recommended reasoning sequence

1. **Question:** State what is to be constructed, calculated, or proved.
2. **Model:** Identify the objects and the geometry in which they live.
3. **Assumptions:** State constraints, idealizations, and permitted operations.
4. **Definitions:** Fix all terms and notation.
5. **Construction:** Give ordered steps or a formal construction.
6. **Derivation:** Show the algebraic, geometric, or logical steps.
7. **Conclusion:** State exactly what has been established.
8. **Verification:** Check dimensions, limiting cases, invariants, and possible degeneracies.

## Common reasoning methods

- Direct construction and proof.
- Congruence and similarity.
- Coordinate geometry and analytic methods.
- Transformations and invariants.
- Locus and intersection arguments.
- Induction or recursion for families of constructions.
- Optimization under stated geometric constraints.

A numerical calculation can help detect an error, but does not replace a general proof when a universal claim is made.
