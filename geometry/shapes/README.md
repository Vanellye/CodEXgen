# Geometrical Shapes

Catalog geometric objects and constructions in a consistent, reusable way.

## Suggested organization

- **2D:** points, lines, angles, polygons, circles, conics, curves, and plane regions.
- **3D:** polyhedra, spheres, cylinders, cones, surfaces, and solids.
- **Higher-dimensional:** n-dimensional objects, projections, cross-sections, and coordinate descriptions.
- **Transformations:** translation, rotation, reflection, scaling, and composition.
- **Properties:** symmetry, proportion, area, perimeter, volume, curvature, and topology where relevant.

## Shape record

For each shape, document its name, definition, dimension, notation, construction, defining properties, formulas with assumptions, and a clearly labeled diagram.

Distinguish exact mathematical objects from approximations or physical examples. Put measurements and plots of real specimens in the relevant observation area, not in this catalog.
