Recursive construction

Increase depth to reveal finer self-similar structure.

The triangle is generated from exact midpoint subdivision. At depth n, 3ⁿ filled triangles remain and their total area is (3/4)ⁿ of the initial area.

Coordinate convention

x = (x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄), xᵢ ∈ {−1, +1}

Vertices are all sign combinations. An edge connects two vertices that differ in exactly one coordinate.

Coordinate-space projection

First two coordinates shown with remaining coordinates encoded by depth and offset.

The 2D view is only a projection of the n-dimensional coordinate object. It cannot preserve all distances and angles once n exceeds two.
